# Privacy Policy

**Last Terminal Update:** February 2026  
**System:** LabGuard Security Infrastructure

---

## 1. Identity Data Collection

LabGuard collects minimal essential data to ensure the integrity of the university infrastructure:

- **Personnel Identity:** Name and institutional email address.
- **Access Logs:** IP addresses, device signatures, and session duration.
- **Security Ciphers:** Encrypted passwords and 2FA secrets.

## 2. Utilization of Data

Data collected is used strictly for the following administrative functions:

- Verification of administrative clearance levels.
- Maintenance of an immutable audit trail for system changes.
- Automated detection of unauthorized access attempts.

## 3. Storage and Encryption

All data is housed on secured servers managed by Araullo University.

- **Encryption:** Sensitive data is hashed using industry-standard Bcrypt and Argon2.
- **Retention:** Records are purged upon formal decommissioning of personnel credentials.

## 4. Disclosure Protocol

LabGuard does not participate in the sale or trade of personnel data. Information is only disclosed if required by university board mandates or national legal compliance.
